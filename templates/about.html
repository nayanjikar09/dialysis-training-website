About Intuit Academy

Specialized Dialysis Technician Training Institute

Who We Are

Intuit Academy is a specialized training institute focused on Dialysis Technology. We train both new students entering the medical field and existing medical staff looking to upskill in dialysis care.

Our Mission

To produce skilled, certified, and job-ready dialysis technicians through hands-on training on real dialysis machines under the guidance of experienced nephrologists and senior technicians.

Who Can Join?

🎓 New Students

12th pass (Science) or equivalent

👩‍⚕️ Nursing Graduates

GNM / B.Sc Nursing holders

🏥 Working Staff

Hospital nurses & technicians seeking upskilling

💼 Dialysis Center Staff

Existing staff seeking formal certification

What You'll Learn

  • 🩸 Kidney anatomy & renal failure basics
  • 🩸 Hemodialysis procedure & machine operation
  • 🩸 Peritoneal dialysis techniques
  • 🩸 Vascular access (fistula, catheter) handling
  • 🩸 Patient monitoring & emergency response
  • 🩸 Infection control & dialyzer reuse protocols

Our Programs

  • 📜 Certificate in Dialysis Technology — 6 Months
  • 📜 Diploma in Dialysis Technology — 1 Year
  • 📜 Advanced Certificate for Medical Staff — 3 Months
  • 📜 Hands-on Dialysis Workshop — 2 Weeks

Why Choose Us

  • ✔ Hands-on training on Fresenius, Nipro & B.Braun machines
  • ✔ Certified by hospitals & dialysis centers
  • ✔ Placement assistance after course completion
  • ✔ Small batches for personalized attention
  • ✔ Taught by practicing nephrologists
